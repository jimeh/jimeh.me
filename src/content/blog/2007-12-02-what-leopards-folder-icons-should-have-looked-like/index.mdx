I don't know about your story, but my story of the first time I saw the new
Leopard folder icons goes something like this; "Wait, what? Is this a joke? Am I
dreaming? WTF?!".

In the end though when Leopard shipped, these new (from a lot aspects uglier)
icons kinda grew on me and I didn't really mind them that much after about a
week. The old Aqua folders unfortunately look horrible as well in Leopard next
to the rest of the UI.

Today I stumbled upon <DeadLink text="Jonas Rask" href="http://jonas.seph.ws/"
/>'s latest work, <DeadLink text="Maji"
href="http://jonas.seph.ws/portfolio/portfolio.html" />.

<Image src={majiPreview} alt="Maji Preview" />

Jonas has done an amazing job with Maji (formally known as "QuickLook") , and
i've fallen in love with the icon set. It's the icons Leopard should have
shipped with in my opinion.

<Image src={myIcons} alt="Home Stack" />

My only wish for Maji is that Jonas makes more icons for the set, and if I could
have a wish, hard drive icons inspired/based on those found in the Agua icon
set.

You can download Maji from <DeadLink text="the linked page"
href="http://jonas.seph.ws/portfolio/portfolio.html" />. Also check out
[CandyBar](http://www.panic.com/candybar/) if you haven't already, makes your
life easy when it comes to installing replacement system icons.
