There is a specific HEX number which has become one hell of a hot topic lately.
It was even the reason for a little scandel over at
[Digg](dead+http://blog.digg.com/?p=74). If you have no clue what i'm talking
about, then i suggest you read [this
post](dead+http://rudd-o.com/archives/2007/04/30/spread-this-number/) over at
[rudd-o.com](dead+http://rudd-o.com/).

Anyway, to get to the point... I had a few minutes of free time last night, and
oddly enough i felt restless and bored, and it was at that moment i stumbled
upon loads of stuff about `09 F9 11 02 9D 74 E3 5B D8 41 56 C5 63 56 88 C0` and
some random page on [cafepress.com](http://www.cafepress.com/). And i suddenly
got an itch to try cafepress out and be a complete uber-geek at the same time :D

So 10 minutes and a fancy styled hex number later, i had this:
[http://www.cafepress.com/zynode](dead+http://www.cafepress.com/zynode)

And then i ordered [one of them](dead+http://www.cafepress.com/zynode.129306565)
for myself, which is now on the way across the planet :D

Oh, and i wrote "freedom of speech" on the back in hex, i used my
little [converter](dead+http://zynode.info/misc/ahb.php)
script for the translation :)
